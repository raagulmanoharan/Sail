Evidence boundary

Sail is a product concept with separate local prototypes and 19 recorded backend tests. No live host integration, external business connection, customer interviews, demand, or unique competitive advantage is claimed. Published capabilities inspired the design direction; they do not prove a market gap.

The access-pause widget uses a synthetic rule: service access pauses now, resumes after the selected duration, and billing is unchanged. Buttons simulate the experience locally; no data leaves this file.
