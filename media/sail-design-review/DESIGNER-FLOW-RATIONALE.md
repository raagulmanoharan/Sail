# Designer flow revision

The screen now follows one document request instead of displaying three equally detailed workflows.

- Business systems form a quiet, single source bar. Availability and commerce remain visible as capabilities without extra miniature screenshots.
- A small Sail mark names the integration layer. Below it, three numbered steps show connected actions, an actual Willow Clinic component and supported-host adapters.
- Two GET examples stay quiet. POST /documents receives the active state and an illustrative 201 received response. Endpoint names are explicitly marked illustrative rather than claiming a shipped connector.
- The component displays a pending state, receives progress and changes to Uploaded for review. This communicates delivery rather than approval or clinical review.
- The existing phone remains mounted and grows to 335px wide in this screen. It is the delivered customer experience, rather than another abstract box.
- One routed request travels from source to Sail. Component progress resolves, then a second signal passes through the adapter into the phone. Inactive lines remain pale.

The motion uses a single existing deterministic GSAP timeline and exact matching path coordinates. All narration and composition duration remain unchanged. Native HTML/SVG only; no generated imagery.
